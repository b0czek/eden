Apps can let the user choose a date and time by touch with `DateTimeField` from `@edenapp/solid-kit/datetime`: steppers for the day, hour, and minute that repeat while held and stay within the given bounds. Its text is in English or Polish, following the system locale, and apps can add languages or replace strings with `addSolidKitStrings` from `@edenapp/solid-kit/locale`.
