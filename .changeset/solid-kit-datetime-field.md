Apps can let the user choose a date and time by touch with `DateTimeField` from `@edenapp/solid-kit/datetime`: steppers for the day, hour, and minute that repeat while held and stay within the given bounds. Solid-kit text follows the app's `@edenapp/babel` locale, in English or Polish, and apps can add languages or change strings under `solidKit` in their own locale files.
