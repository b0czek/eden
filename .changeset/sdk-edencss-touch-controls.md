EdenCSS controls are touch-friendly. Buttons, inputs, and selects are at least `--eden-layout-control-min-size` (44px) high, and buttons are at least that wide at every size. `.eden-btn-square` is exactly that size. Hover styles apply only on devices that can hover, and buttons, list items, sidebar items, tabs, and interactive cards show a pressed state.
