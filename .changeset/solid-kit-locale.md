"@edenapp/solid-kit": minor
"@edenapp/babel": minor
---

Solid-kit components show their built-in text in the app's `@edenapp/babel` language, with English and Polish included. Apps add languages or change strings under `solidKit` in their own locale files. Dialogs no longer default to English buttons. `NoticeProvider` no longer takes `labels`, `Sidebar` no longer takes `toggleLabels`, and `KeyboardButton` no longer takes `label`.
