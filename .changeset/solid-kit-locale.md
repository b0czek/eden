"@edenapp/solid-kit": minor
"@edenapp/babel": minor
---

Solid-kit components show their built-in text in the app's `@edenapp/babel` language, with English and Polish included: dialogs no longer default to English buttons, and notices, sidebars, and the keyboard button no longer need labels. Apps add languages or change strings under `solidKit` in their own locale files, and can still pass labels to one component.
